SimpleTxtReader Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

SimpleTxtReader ("the app") is built so that your reading stays yours. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • Your books, reading position, preferences, and bookmarks are stored only on your device.
  • We do not have servers that receive or store your reading data.

What SimpleTxtReader Stores, and Where

All of the following is saved locally on your device only, using standard iOS storage (app preferences and the app's local file storage). It is never transmitted to us or any third party by the app itself:

  • The text of books you open
  • Your reading position, bookmarks, and search history within the app
  • Display preferences such as font, text size, and colors

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your personal backup like any other app data — that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Network Requests You Initiate

SimpleTxtReader only makes a network request when you explicitly choose to:

  • Opening a web link — if you paste a URL into "Open Web Link," the app downloads that page's content directly from the address you provided, so it can be displayed in the reader. We do not see, log, or store which links you open; the request goes straight from your device to the website you specified.

No other network activity happens in the app outside of the optional tip jar purchase flow described below.

Optional Tips

SimpleTxtReader offers an optional, one-time tip to support development. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit — SimpleTxtReader never receives your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase and billing information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Text-to-Speech

The optional audiobook feature uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to read text aloud. This processing happens entirely on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Children's Privacy

SimpleTxtReader does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.
