SimpleTxtReader Support

A clean, distraction-free reader for TXT, HTML, EPUB, and PDB files.

About SimpleTxtReader

SimpleTxtReader is a free iPhone and iPad app for reading long-form text comfortably. Open a book from the Files app or download text straight from a web link, and SimpleTxtReader shows it as clean, scrollable paragraphs in the font, size, and colors you prefer. There are no accounts, ads, or clutter.

It's built for the things other readers often handle poorly: plain .txt novels, older PalmDOC ebooks, and web pages you'd rather read without the surrounding site. Text encodings are detected automatically, so books in Chinese (GB18030/GBK, Big5), Japanese, and other languages open without garbled characters. Fonts such as PingFang and Hiragino are included for comfortable CJK reading.

When you'd rather listen, SimpleTxtReader can read the book aloud using on-device speech, picking up from wherever you are.

Getting Started

  1. Open a book. Tap the document icon in the top-left corner and choose Open File to pick a book from Files, iCloud Drive, or any connected storage provider.
  2. Or open a web link. Choose Open Web Link, paste the address of a text file or web page, and tap Download TXT. SimpleTxtReader downloads the page and keeps only its readable text.
  3. Make it yours. Tap the text-size icon in the top-right corner to change the font, text size, and colors.
  4. Read. Scroll to read. Your place is saved automatically, and the last book you opened is waiting for you next time.

Ways to Use It

  • Reading downloaded novels. Open .txt, .epub, or .pdb books you've saved to Files, and read them in a consistent, distraction-free layout.
  • Following web serials and chapter lists. Open a web page that lists chapters, then tap the menu button in the reading toolbar. Under Links from page, tap any chapter to download and read it. Use the HTML Links option in Reading Options to list links first-to-last or last-to-first, so the newest chapter is easy to find.
  • Listening hands-free. Turn on audiobook narration in Reading Options, then use the play, pause, and stop buttons in the reading toolbar. Speed, pitch, and volume are adjustable, and narration starts from your current place.
  • Studying or referencing a text. Search the whole book, step through every match, and bookmark passages you want to come back to.

Supported Formats

  • Plain text (.txt)
  • HTML pages (.html, .htm)
  • EPUB (.epub)
  • PDB / PalmDOC ebooks (.pdb)

Note: some .pdb files use proprietary, non-standard formats that SimpleTxtReader can't read. If one fails to open, it likely isn't in the standard PalmDOC format.

Reading Options

Tap the text-size icon in the top-right corner to customize your reading experience:

  • Text and background colors
  • Text size, from 12 to 36pt
  • A choice of fonts, including Serif, Monospaced, Rounded, Georgia, and more
  • Audiobook narration, read aloud on-device with adjustable speed, pitch, and volume
  • Display order for links extracted from imported HTML pages

Bookmarks & Search

Use the bookmark button in the reading toolbar to save your place, and the menu button to jump back to any saved bookmark. Use the search bar to find text within the current book and step through matches.

SimpleTxtReader remembers the paragraph at the top of the screen, so you return to exactly where you left off. While audiobook narration is playing or paused, your place follows the paragraph being read aloud.

Support Development

SimpleTxtReader is completely free, with no ads, subscriptions, or paywalls. If you'd like to support future updates, an optional one-time tip is available from Reading Options — entirely optional.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is my data private? Yes — all books, bookmarks, and settings stay on your device. SimpleTxtReader doesn't require an account and doesn't track you.
  • Why won't my file open? Double-check the file extension matches a supported format above. Some ebook files use custom, non-standard variants that aren't readable.
  • Why does my EPUB say it's damaged or too large? Rather than opening a book with chapters silently missing, SimpleTxtReader stops and tells you when an EPUB can't be read in full. This happens if a chapter is missing or corrupted inside the file, if a single section is larger than 64 MB, or if the whole book contains more than 200 MB of text. Try downloading the book again, or getting another copy of it.
  • Will I lose my bookmarks when I update the app? No. Bookmarks from earlier versions are kept. If a bookmark doesn't appear right away, open the same book again from the file menu and it will be linked back to that book.
  • Can I read books across devices? Not currently — books and bookmarks are stored locally on each device.
