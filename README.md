## About the app

SimpleTxtReader makes long-form text comfortable to read. Open a book from the Files app or download text straight from a web link, and it appears as clean, scrollable paragraphs in the font, size, and colors you choose. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking, and everything stays on your device.

### Features

- **Many formats:** plain text (`.txt`), HTML (`.html`, `.htm`), EPUB (`.epub`), and PalmDOC ebooks (`.pdb`).
- **Web import:** paste a link to download a text file or web page and keep only its readable text.
- **Chapter navigation for web pages:** links found on an imported page are listed in the menu, so you can move through a chapter index one tap at a time.
- **Automatic encoding detection:** Chinese (GB18030/GBK, Big5), Japanese, and other non-Latin texts open without garbled characters.
- **Reading options:** 22 fonts (including PingFang and Hiragino for CJK text), text sizes from 12 to 36 pt, and custom text and background colors.
- **Audiobook narration:** on-device text-to-speech with adjustable speed, pitch, and volume.
- **Search and bookmarks:** find text anywhere in the book, step through matches, and bookmark passages.
- **Remembers your place:** reopens the last book at the paragraph where you left off.
- **Free:** no ads, subscriptions, or paywalls. An optional one-time tip supports development.

## How to use it

1. **Open a book.** Tap the document icon (top left) and choose **Open File**, or choose **Open Web Link**, paste an address, and tap **Download TXT**.
2. **Adjust the look.** Tap the text-size icon (top right) to change the font, size, and colors, or to turn on narration.
3. **Read or listen.** Scroll to read. With narration on, use play, pause, and stop in the reading toolbar.
4. **Find and mark passages.** Use the search bar to find text, and the bookmark button to save your place.
5. **Jump around.** Tap the menu button in the reading toolbar to open your bookmarks or, for web pages, the links found on that page.

See the [support page](https://s544v2nj4s.github.io/SimpleTxtReader-Support/) for more detail and FAQs.
